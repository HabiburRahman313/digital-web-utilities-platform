Voice & Tone Notes
(For future copywriting — human or AI-assisted, per AI_Prompts.md Section 10 & 21)
•	Direct, warm, confident — never salesy or hype-driven. No fake urgency, no inflated claims, no fabricated stats or user counts.
•	Privacy and speed are the two recurring pillars — nearly every piece of copy above touches at least one. Keep reinforcing both consistently rather than introducing new claims per page.
•	"No sign-up / no uploads / no limits" is the closest thing to a recurring brand mantra — reuse this three-beat rhythm in new copy where it fits naturally, rather than inventing new variations each time.
•	Avoid generic SaaS clichés ("revolutionize," "game-changing," "next-generation") — the brand's strength is plain, credible usefulness, not hype.
•	Numbers (e.g. "100+ tools") should be updated here first as the platform grows, then propagated to every page listed above — this file is the source of truth, not any individual page.
